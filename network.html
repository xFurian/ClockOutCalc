System Overview

Orbi 770 is a tri-band WiFi 7 mesh platform designed to provide whole-home wireless coverage while retaining multi-gigabit wired connectivity.

Platform

NETGEAR Orbi 770 Series

Tri-band WiFi 7 mesh system using 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz wireless bands.

Wireless

WiFi 7 / BE11000

Supports WiFi 7 features including Multi-Link Operation, 320 MHz channels, and 4K-QAM.

Topology

Mesh Networking

Router-and-satellite architecture with wireless or optional multi-gig Ethernet backhaul.

Wireless Architecture

2.4 GHz

Longer-Range Coverage

Lower-frequency wireless connectivity for compatible clients and broader home coverage.

5 GHz

High-Speed Client Access

High-throughput wireless connectivity for modern devices, streaming, gaming, and general network traffic.

6 GHz

WiFi 7 Capacity

Higher-bandwidth WiFi 7 connectivity for compatible 6 GHz clients and reduced congestion.

Multi-Link Operation Orbi 770 supports WiFi 7 Multi-Link Operation, allowing compatible connections to make use of multiple wireless links to improve throughput, reliability, and latency characteristics.
